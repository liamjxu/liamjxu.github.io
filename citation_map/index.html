Methodology. Citation data comes from my Google Scholar profile (264 total citations across 12 works; 262 citation entries, corresponding to 246 unique citing papers — a citing paper may cite more than one work). Author affiliations for each citing paper were retrieved from OpenAlex, an open scholarly index. 185 of 246 unique citing papers (75%) were matched, of which 85 carried institution/country metadata — so the map understates true geographic reach (preprints often lack affiliation data). A country is credited when at least one author of a citing paper is affiliated with an institution there. Counts are unique citing papers. Independent citations are those with no author overlap with my co-author network (225 of 262 entries).
